Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Who we are

Harb Al-Maarifa: Battle of Minds (حرب المعرفة) is run by OptiVault, a company registered in Wyoming, United States, which is responsible for your data.

For anything about your data: jebariayman8@gmail.com

What we collect

  • The name you choose in the game, your avatar, and your country if you choose one.
  • Your game progress and match statistics: trophies, level, gold, pearls, items, quests and achievements.
  • Your friends, the players you blocked, and the reports you send or that are sent about your name.
  • Your answers to the daily question.
  • Your App Store purchases: Apple's transaction id, the product and the date. We never see your card or your Apple account.
  • Technical server logs, which include IP addresses, kept for 30 days.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or your device's advertising identifier.

Why we use it

  • To run the game, online matches and matchmaking.
  • For the leaderboards and the countries league.
  • To deliver and restore purchases and to handle refunds.
  • To prevent cheating and protect players: the name filter, reports and blocks.
  • To answer your messages to support.

Legal bases

We use your data to perform our agreement with you when you play (the terms), and for our legitimate interest in preventing cheating and protecting players. Reminders are notifications on your own device that work with your consent; you can turn them off in Settings.

Who else sees it

  • Google Cloud hosts the game's servers in Doha, Qatar, with daily backups in the European Union.
  • Apple processes payments through the App Store.
  • Other players see your name, avatar, level and rank in matches and on leaderboards.

There are no ads and no tracking in the game. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Transfers out of your country

Data is stored in Qatar with backups in the European Union. If you are in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates or another country, this transfers your data out of your country; we do so as Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law and the UAE's Personal Data Protection Law allow, and apply the same protection wherever it is.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete it. After deletion we keep purchase records with nothing that identifies you, to handle refunds and for accounting. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data, and object to its use.

To delete your account: Settings › الحساب › حذف الحساب, or write to jebariayman8@gmail.com. We answer every request within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The connection to the server is encrypted (HTTPS), we store a hash of your sign-in token rather than the token itself, and only the people who run the servers can reach them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the game if the change matters.
